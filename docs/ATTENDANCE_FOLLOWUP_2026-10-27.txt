Attendance follow-up — 27 October 2026

Changes
1. Linked teachers with attendance.self now land on Punch in & out from the root/login route. Overview remains available at /overview. Explicit deep links remain usable; school owners and non-teacher roles retain their existing landing rules. Demo navigation keeps its original root route.
2. School settings > Staff & Payroll > Work & Leave now has optional Half day after. An arrival strictly after this local school time counts as half-day, even if minimum working hours are later met. Exact cutoff time is not after it. The existing minimum-minutes rule still also applies. Empty disables the arrival cutoff. Valid cutoff is at/after Late after and before school end. Each punch captures its work-rule snapshot; later settings changes do not rewrite that day. Manual time corrections apply the same rule. Approved ARS still explicitly credits the requested period.
3. Future pending leave requests appear with a small pink dot in the personal calendar. Selecting a date in the request opens Cancel leave request with its date range/reason. Confirmation cancels the entire request and refreshes the calendar. The same dialog is reused by the leave list. Approved, rejected, cancelled and already-started requests cannot be cancelled this way; backend authorization, revisions and payroll locks remain authoritative.

No environment, dependency or database changes. No migration is required. Existing uncommitted changes in both repositories were preserved.
