Teacher calendar and ARS refinement — 27 October 2026

Delivered behavior
- Personal attendance calendar shows date numbers only. The six legends are Late (yellow), On leave (pink), Absent (red), Half day (purple), School holiday (orange), and Invalid (black). Half-day leave uses the same purple appearance. Weekly holidays follow the school's configured working days and have no extra legend.
- Selecting any date shows its punch-in, punch-out and working hours. Only an eligible Half day or Invalid attendance record opens ARS; pending requests cannot be duplicated. Future, absent, present, late-only and unmarked dates do not open the request form.
- ARS contains the selected date/status/punch details, Full day / First half / Second half, and Forgot to punch out / Forgot to punch in / App issue / Network issue / Other reason. No free date picker or salary-treatment controls remain in the live attendance correction flow.
- Pending and Past requests are separate tabs. Past requests follow the selected month; pending requests remain visible across months.
- An unfinished physical punch becomes Invalid after the school's local midnight. Entering Punch in & out automatically asks for ARS for the oldest outstanding missed punch. Submission closes that unresolved punch and permits today's punch before approval, subject to normal location/workday checks. The previous day remains Invalid until approved. Multiple missed days must each be addressed.
- Approval applies the requested period, not an unrelated status selected by the reviewer. A full day credits school start to end; halves split that interval at its midpoint. For 08:00–15:00, first half is 08:00–11:30 and second half is 11:30–15:00. Existing per-day work-rule snapshots take precedence over later setting changes.
- Approved ARS replaces the displayed late classification. This supersedes the earlier attendance report's behavior that retained the late indicator after approval.
- Administrators can correct local punch-in/out times with a reason; duration, late status and full/half-day classification recalculate. Original physical punches and correction history remain available for audit. Credited times/hours are clearly identified in the teacher's detail view.
- Attendance approval does not ask about pay or create automatic salary deductions. Existing payroll, leave and payment workflows remain separate. Payroll locking, revision checks, no self-approval, tenant isolation, attendance permissions and CSRF enforcement are preserved.
- Festival names are deferred as requested; the calendar currently displays School holiday for configured holiday dates.

Implementation and API dependencies
- Existing request and decision APIs now accept/store requestedPeriod and reasonCode. Legacy request fields remain accepted for compatibility.
- Added PUT /api/v1/schools/:schoolId/hr/staff/:id/attendance-times (attendance.manage + CSRF), with date, revision, local punchIn/punchOut HH:mm, and reason.
- Added optional embedded credited-time/correction fields to existing attendance records. No collection reset, index change, migration or initialization command is required.
- Midnight status is derived on reads; there is no midnight write sweep. The punch page refreshes once on school-date rollover, rather than making API calls every second. Browser clocks do not authorize punches: server checks remain authoritative.
- No environment, dependency, credential, production database, or school-record changes were made for this task. Runtime browser/database checks used disposable local MongoDB fixtures only.

Try the flow
1. Restart backend and refresh the frontend in live mode. No database script is needed.
2. Teacher: My attendance & salary > Attendance. Select a Half day or Invalid date, choose ARS type/reason and submit.
3. Teacher: ARS > Pending. The old day remains Invalid until a reviewer approves it.
4. Administrator: staff attendance > ARS requests. Review the requested period, enter a decision reason and approve or reject. Salary treatment is absent from this flow.
5. Teacher: refresh attendance or reopen it. Approval shows the accepted period and approved hours, without a stale Late label. Past requests contains the decision.
6. Administrator: use the attendance row's correction action to edit local punch times with a reason. Reopening the teacher calendar/profile reflects the recalculated values.
7. A real unfinished punch from yesterday automatically opens ARS on today's Punch in & out page. Submitting it permits today's punch before approval; location checks still apply.
