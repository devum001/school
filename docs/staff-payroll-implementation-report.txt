STAFF PAYROLL AND TEACHER ATTENDANCE — IMPLEMENTATION REPORT

Scope
Implemented live staff salary setup, effective-month salary and attendance policies,
manual attendance decisions, teacher location-based punch-in/out, leave requests and
paid/unpaid review, monthly payroll drafts and approval, partial/full payments,
advance issuance/recovery, outstanding balances, and printable salary statements.
Existing staff entry and Excel import remain the source of staff identities.
Existing demo screens and their local repository have not been replaced.
The new approval/punch workflow is live-only; the demo retains its existing salary
and attendance behavior. There is no live fallback to local demo records.

Where to use it
- Staff & HR: existing directory and import, plus Attendance, Leave and Salary Expenses.
- Finance (/fees): salary workspace; /attendance/staff: staff attendance review.
- School settings > Staff & Payroll: Departments, Work & Leave, Payroll.
- My attendance & salary (/staff/self): linked account's own records and punch controls.
- Roles & Permissions: six explicit new capabilities, independently assignable.
Teacher accounts need attendance.self and an active membership. Payroll managers
need payroll.view and payroll.manage. Approval and payment permissions are separate.
School account linking also requires memberships.view and memberships.manage.
Super Admin access does not bypass school membership/tenant checks.

Financial behavior
Amounts are integer paise. Salary period is a calendar month; payment due day is in
the following month. A due date never creates a payment or marks salary paid.
Joining/leaving prorates monthly earnings using calendar employment days.
Unpaid-day deduction uses configured basic/gross basis and calendar/working/30 divisor.
Missed punches, unmarked attendance and unresolved leave block approval, rather than
automatically reducing pay. Admins explicitly choose paid/unpaid attendance treatment.
Approval is available after the salary month ends and freezes its financial snapshot.
Changes to approved periods require an adjustment in an open period.
Payment records are append-only, idempotent on retry, and cannot exceed outstanding pay.
Advance recovery is bounded by the outstanding advance ledger; approved payroll reserves
that recovery even while its salary payment is outstanding.
These screens record payments already made. They do not send money through a bank.

Attendance and leave
School-specific campus centres and radii: 25–1,000 metres, up to ten locations.
Fresh device coordinates and accuracy are checked against the school area; the accuracy
circle must fit inside the radius. Server time and school timezone determine the day.
Only linked, active teaching staff with punch attendance enabled may punch.
One in/out pair per local day; incomplete/late/short attendance requires review.
Original punch events remain separate from administrative attendance decisions.
A person cannot approve their own leave or attendance correction.
Leave requests are prospective; the reviewer chooses paid or unpaid. The current paid
allowance is measured per calendar year, not per academic year. Approved charge dates
and paid days are retained when work-day settings subsequently change.
Default start/end/late/half-day rules and paid allowance follow the existing demo values
(08:00–15:00, 08:15, 210 minutes, 18 paid days). Configure school policy before use.
The live version currently enforces one combined paid allowance; the demo's separate
casual/sick quotas and academic-year leave-limit configuration are not connected to it.

Security and persistence
Every write rechecks school membership and explicit permission grants inside the
existing school-authorization transaction. CSRF checks apply to all HR mutations.
Staff.view alone does not expose salary data. Self-service filters to the linked staff ID.
Revision checks protect configuration, decisions and payroll drafts against lost updates.
Draft approval detects changes in calculation inputs. Audit events record every mutation.
ObjectIds are serialized as strings; errors expose only fixed public messages.
Read requests use the existing scoped, coalesced cache; writes invalidate reads.
Date/month indexes support the actual HR queries. No per-row frontend polling is added.
School access/role behavior remains based on stored grants; an initial owner bypass was
caught by regression tests and removed.
A read-only readiness check rejects HR requests if the declared indexes are missing.
Application startup does NOT create HR collections or change permissions.

The first command reports collection presence; it is not a full duplicate-index audit.
The second creates the eight new HR collections and declared indexes; it does not
remove existing collections, records or indexes. Index conflicts fail explicitly.
The third explicitly grants the six HR permissions to owner roles of active schools,
with operator audit events. Other role permissions are not changed. Re-running this
explicit grant command can restore HR permissions deliberately removed from an owner.
New schools already receive the current explicit owner permission catalog.
Review these commands before applying them to the configured database.
Then restart the backend and refresh/re-sign-in so membership permissions refresh.

and enable punch attendance with its effective month.

geolocation is available. Keep exact allowed origins and secure authentication settings.

Limits and remaining work
GPS is a convenience control, not spoof-proof proof of physical presence. No biometric,
device-attestation or continuous tracking system is implemented. Use admin review for
location failures. Overnight/multiple-shift attendance needs a separate workflow.
The HR workspace has explicit bounds: 500 staff, 16,000 daily records/month, 2,000 leave
records in the selected month, and 5,000 advance/payment records in its result scope.
It fails clearly instead of returning a silently truncated financial ledger. Large-school
pagination and measured production load testing remain future work; no latency SLA claimed.
The existing demo has not been upgraded to the new live approval/payment model.
This implementation does not calculate statutory tax, PF/ESI, bank transfers or automatic
payment reversals. Payment corrections need an explicit audited reversal workflow before
allowing operators to amend an incorrectly entered payment.
Existing demo document-template customization is not yet used by the live printable
salary statement. The statement is an explicit live calculation/payment record.
The live staff import continues importing existing profile columns; salary configuration
is individual, and is not silently inferred from Excel or converted into login accounts.

Approval boundary
Automatic approval review rejected adding collection/index creation to startup because
that could mutate the configured database under the prior no-migrations restriction.
That rejected startup change was not applied. The reviewable alternative is the separate
manual initialization and explicit permission-grant commands above. They require approval
before execution against the configured database.
