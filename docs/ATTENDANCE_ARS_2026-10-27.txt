Automatic teacher attendance and correction requests — 27 October 2026

Behavior
- Punch-in is attendance, separate from signing into the application. Signing out
  does not create a punch-out. Location verification still applies to both punches.
- A normal school day does not need administrative review. Arrival after the
  school's Late after time is immediately Late. Working time below Full-day minimum
  produces Half day at punch-out; otherwise the day is Present/Late. Late remains
  visible independently of the full/half-day outcome, including after a correction.
- Example: 08:00–15:00, late after 08:15, minimum 210 minutes. A noon arrival is
  Late immediately. Leaving at 15:00 gives 180 minutes: Half day + Late. Staying
  until 15:30 meets 210 minutes: Late/full day. Neither automatically deducts pay.
- Punch-in snapshots that day's timing rules and school timezone. Later edits to
  Work & Leave Rules apply to subsequent punches; they do not rewrite that shift.
- Admin reviewing an open day no longer prevents the teacher's real punch-out.
  Actual timestamps are recorded while the explicit attendance/pay decision stays.
- Missing a previous day's punch-out blocks a new punch-in until an ARS is submitted.
  ARS means Attendance Regularization Request. Submission allows the next working
  day's punch-in immediately; the old day stays Invalid until approval.
- An ARS asks for Full day or Half day and a reason. Teachers can request a past
  employed working day, or today after school hours. No future dates. They cannot
  approve their own request even if their role otherwise manages attendance.
- The admin approves/rejects in Attendance > Staff Attendance > Attendance requests.
  Approval chooses full/half day and paid/no deduction or an explicit half-day
  deduction. Rejection keeps Invalid and allows resubmission. Pending requests
  cannot be bypassed by manual attendance correction. Approved payroll is locked.
- No punch-out time is invented by an ARS. Raw arrival/departure and request history
  remain preserved. Corrections are audited and guarded by school tenancy, current
  permissions, CSRF and revision checks. Retried request keys do not duplicate ARS.
- In attendance-based payroll, unresolved missing punches/ARS block approval until
  resolved and recalculated. Salary-only staff retain their existing workflow.

Screens / how to test
1. As school admin: School settings > Staff & Payroll > Work & Leave Rules.
   Confirm school timings, cutoff, full-day minimum, working days and campus radius.
2. As a linked teacher with Own attendance and salary: open Punch in & out.
   Punch in at school. Check immediate On duty/Late status and the live local clock.
3. Admin: Attendance > Staff Attendance. Check in/out chips, Late badge, filters,
   and On duty count. Open the staff name for profile and monthly attendance history.
4. Review today's attendance while it remains open. Teacher can still punch out
   from an allowed school location; the counter stops after a successful punch-out.
5. For a missed old punch: teacher opens Punch in & out > Request correction.
   Submit the affected date and full/half day. Confirm Invalid while pending, then
   verify that today's punch-in is allowed without waiting for admin approval.
6. Admin reviews the request in the attendance inbox. Teacher refreshes requests or
   opens My attendance & salary to see the accepted day and calendar color. Profile
   late counts continue to show actual late arrivals. Pending requests from older
   months stay in the inbox. Monthly history is available through self-service.

Deployment / data
- Restart/redeploy both backend and frontend together. No new database script,
  collection, index, credential or environment variable is required by this change.
- New optional attendance fields and embedded request history use the existing
  staff/day collection. No real database records have been rewritten or deleted.
- Existing missed punches are identified on read. Existing explicit admin decisions
  are preserved. Historical Review rows are not guessed into new attendance outcomes;
  use an explicit correction when needed.
- Work-rule calendar history is not versioned globally; new punch timings are
  snapshotted per day. Leave/holiday edits retain the existing app's behavior.
- Verification uses disposable local MongoDB replica sets, not Atlas. Device GPS
  accuracy in a real campus must still be checked with your device and saved radius.
