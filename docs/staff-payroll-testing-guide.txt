STAFF & PAYROLL — MANUAL TEST FLOW

Use a test school and clearly labelled test accounts. Payment entries record money
actually paid; there is no payment-reversal workflow yet. Avoid test payments in a
real school's books. The automated browser tests use a disposable local database,
not your configured school database.

1. ADMIN: CONFIGURE SCHOOL RULES
Sign in as the school owner.
School settings > Staff & Payroll:
- Departments: Add Department to create a name; each row has Edit and Delete. Click a department
  to see its staff. Assign that department when creating/editing a staff record.
- Work & Leave: Edit Details. Configure timings, working days, leave allowance,
  holidays and the campus centre latitude/longitude and permitted radius.
- Payroll: Edit Details. Choose the next-month payment due day and deduction basis.
Expected: values persist after refresh. No salary month selector in school-wide rules.

2. ADD TWO TEST STAFF
Staff & HR > All Staff > Add Staff (or Import staff with the existing template).
A: Teaching staff, with the teaching checkbox selected.
B: Non-teaching, salary-only staff.
Use unique staff codes and truthful joining dates for the test scenario.
Expected: records appear in the directory. Teaching staff also appear under Teachers.

3. CREATE THE TEACHER LOGIN WITHIN THE SCHOOL
A staff profile is not itself a login.
- School settings > Roles & Permissions: create/edit a Teacher role with
  "Own attendance and salary". Do not grant payroll management or approval.
- Teachers or Staff & HR > All Staff: click Enable login next to the teacher.
  Enter their login email and password and select the Teacher role.
  Saving creates the account, active school membership and staff link together.
  No Super Admin step is needed.
- Staff & HR > Salary Expenses > the teacher's Salary setup:
  select the effective month, enter salary, select "Teacher punch in / out" and save.
  The login is already linked.
- Set the other staff member to "Salary only — no attendance".
Expected: the linked teacher has access to their own records, not everyone's salary.
An email belonging to an existing account cannot be overwritten or taken over here.
For an existing account, use the existing school membership/link workflow.
Manual attendance is available when an administrator maintains the register.

4. TEACHER: PUNCH IN AND OUT
Use a separate browser profile/private window to sign in as the teacher.
Open My attendance & salary (/staff/self).
Allow browser location access while inside the configured campus.
Punch in, then punch out at the end of work.
Expected:
- An accurate, fresh location inside the campus is accepted.
- Outside-campus or inaccurate location is rejected with a message.
- Repeated/out-of-order punches are rejected.
- A very short test shift can show half-day / pending; this is expected.
- Signing out of the app does NOT punch out.
On a phone, use HTTPS; visiting a laptop's plain HTTP LAN address cannot reliably
use browser geolocation. Browser GPS is not an anti-spoofing guarantee.

5. LEAVE AND ATTENDANCE REVIEW
Teacher > My attendance & salary > Request leave:
select a future working date, leave type, duration and reason.
Admin > Staff & HR > Leave:
review the request; approve/reject and choose Paid or Unpaid with a reason.
Admin > Staff & HR > Attendance:
choose the date; Review / correct the teacher's record and decide salary treatment.
Expected:
- The request starts pending. The teacher cannot approve their own request.
- Paid leave consumes the annual allowance; unpaid leave affects salary deductions.
- Late/short/missing punches need admin review; they do not silently deduct salary.
- Refresh both accounts to see the saved decision.

6. PREPARE AND APPROVE SALARY
Admin > Staff & HR > Salary Expenses (also available through Finance):
select the salary month and department, then Prepare salary for a staff member.
Review salary components, adjustments and any advance recovery. Save the draft.
Resolve attendance warnings, then Recalculate draft if inputs changed.
Review & approve freezes the calculation.
Expected:
- Payroll cannot be approved before the salary month ends.
- Unresolved attendance blocks approval.
- Approval does not mark the salary paid.
To test this entire sequence immediately, use a completed month in a TEST school:
the staff joining date and salary effective month must cover that month.
For a simple payment test use salary-only staff; for attendance-based payroll every
required day in that period must be resolved. Do not change your computer's clock
or falsify real staff history to bypass approval rules.

7. PARTIAL/FULL PAYMENTS AND STATEMENT
For a test net salary of ₹30,000:
- Record payment ₹10,000 with date, method and reference:
  status becomes Partially paid; outstanding becomes ₹20,000.
- Record the remaining ₹20,000:
  status becomes Paid; outstanding becomes zero.
- Open Statement to inspect components, deductions, payments and balance.
  Print or save as PDF.
Expected:
- Overpayment is rejected. Refresh preserves the balance.
- Payment due date alone never changes paid status.
- Recording a payment does not transfer money through a bank.

8. OPTIONAL ADVANCE CHECK
Record an advance with amount, date, method and reason.
Prepare a later salary draft with an advance recovery amount.
Expected: recovery cannot exceed the outstanding advance; an approved payroll
reserves that recovery. Advance history remains available.

9. DEMO REGRESSION
Run a separate frontend dev server with VITE_APP_MODE=demo and an unused port.
Demo continues to use local storage and its original salary workflow.
Live mode does not fall back to demo data.

CURRENT LIMITATIONS
There is no bank integration, payroll payment reversal, biometric/device-attestation
system, statutory tax/PF/ESI calculation or overnight shift workflow.
Live paid leave is a combined calendar-year allowance, not the demo's separate
casual/sick academic-year quotas.
This guide describes manual acceptance checks; it does not claim that real GPS
hardware or all these steps were exercised against your actual school.
