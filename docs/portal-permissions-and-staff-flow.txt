Portal permissions and staff flow — 20 October 2026

What the permission catalogue actually covers
The backend exposes 27 school permissions:
- Memberships: view/manage
- Roles: view/manage
- School profile: view/manage
- Academic years: view/manage
- Classes, sections, subjects: view/manage for each
- Staff, students, teaching assignments: view/manage for each
- Audit: view
- Own attendance and salary: self-service (also leave requests)
- Attendance: manage (includes reviewing attendance and leave)
- Payroll: view, manage, approve, pay
These are not individual switches for every demo feature or button. Departments and
work/payroll rules share existing HR permissions; leave approval shares attendance
management. Notifications, examinations/marks workflows and other unconnected demo
features do not gain authority from imaginary permissions. They remain in demo;
placeholder-only areas are no longer offered as usable live navigation.

Role editor
The editor displays every capability returned by the backend across View and Manage
tabs. Own attendance and salary is in View; salary approval and payment are explicit
Manage choices. Both tabs preserve selections. Selecting a manage capability includes
its matching view capability when one exists. Removing that view removes dependent
manage choices. Delegation remains restricted to the actor's granted permissions.
Backend checks remain authoritative. Hidden links do not replace authorization.

Live navigation
The shared shell accepts a route-visibility filter; demo defaults remain unchanged.
Students, Teachers/Staff, Finance and staff attendance appear only for users who can
read/use those areas. Unsupported live placeholder navigation is hidden. Inaccessible
routes redirect to the user's available landing area. Account settings remain available.
Settings tabs/subtabs also use the actual view/manage permissions. Teacher self-service
users see their own attendance/salary link and account settings, not school-wide records.
Unavailable notifications and unauthorized student search are removed from live controls.

Staff flow
Add staff/teacher -> save profile -> salary and attendance setup opens directly.
Setup may be closed and finished later. Existing records have Salary & attendance in
the directory. The monthly salary area also offers Enable login for eligible unlinked
staff, so creating their account no longer requires returning to a separate screen.
Monthly workflow stays Prepare salary -> review/approve -> Record payment -> Statement.
Payment records represent actual money paid; a simple paid flag would lose partial
payments, balances and audit history. Advances are under More actions.
Teacher directory and its HR tabs are restricted to teaching staff. Staff & HR remains
the combined directory, including teachers. Department CRUD remains in school settings.

Personal attendance
The self-service endpoint already returns only the linked user's records; it is still
used exclusively. Responsive seven-column monthly calendar, coloured statuses, text
labels, legend, keyboard-focusable days and details on selection. Explicit absent,
late, half-day, review, leave, half-day leave, holidays, off days, unmarked and upcoming
states are distinct. Missing records are not invented absences. Leave snapshots use
approvedDates where present. Salary is shown in a compact personal card with Statement.

Login/logout
Branded forest/cream sign-in layout using existing primitives, responsive layout,
password visibility toggle and existing secure session flow. Logout remains an action
with a progress state, followed by the sign-in screen. No authentication storage or
backend security behavior was replaced.

Try the flow
1. As school owner, open Roles & Permissions; create a role using View/Manage.
2. Add a teacher. Save the profile, then complete salary and attendance setup.
3. Use Enable login for the teacher and assign the intended school role.
4. Sign in as that teacher in a separate browser profile. Open My attendance & salary.
5. As owner, prepare the monthly salary, review/approve and record actual payment.
   Outstanding balance and payment status follow the recorded amounts.
Existing demo-only modules still require backend implementation before live access.
